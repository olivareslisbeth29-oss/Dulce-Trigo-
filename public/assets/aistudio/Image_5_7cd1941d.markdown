## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the warmth, slow-craft heritage, and sensory comfort of a traditional neighborhood bakery combined with frictionless modern digital hospitality. Designed for tactile in-store QR dining, mobile dessert browsing, and takeout orders, the visual atmosphere balances organic warmth with direct transactional clarity.

The aesthetic fuses **Warm Editorial Craft** with **Modern Tactile Minimalism**:
- **Warmth & Hearth:** Rich natural wheat tones, toasted caramel, and deep espresso inks anchored by an authentic linen/cream canvas.
- **Sensory Elegance:** Evocative editorial serif headlines that evoke golden crusts, flaky textures, and family recipes, paired with clean, highly readable grotesk body typography.
- **Modern Hospitality:** Clear ordering pathways, tactile pill badges, smooth interactive sheet surfaces, and distinct WhatsApp-friendly order confirmations.

## Colors

The color palette reflects the baking process: flour, browned butter, proofing dough, and oven-baked crust.

- **Primary (`#D97706` - Golden Amber):** The signature tone of golden crusts and baked honey. Used for interactive accents, highlight tags, primary actions, and badge accents.
- **Secondary (`#372A20` - Roast Espresso):** The primary text and deep container tone. Replaces harsh synthetic blacks with a rich, roasted-coffee bean dark brown that anchors cards, hero blocks, and footer sections.
- **Tertiary (`#B45309` - Toasted Caramel):** Deeper amber used for pressed states, active menu category indicators, and price accents.
- **Neutral (`#FDF6ED` - Warm Linen / Harina):** The base background surface across all views, eliminating sterile white glare while providing ample contrast.
- **Named Accents:**
  - `whatsapp-green` (`#22C55E`): Dedicated purely to direct WhatsApp ordering, confirmation tags, and live chat assistance.
  - `surface-espresso` (`#2A1F17`): Deep feature callout cards and persistent cart chips.
  - `border-warm` (`#E7DACB`): Subtle border separator that harmonizes naturally with the cream foundation.

## Typography

The typographic pairing reflects the intersection of classical bakery heritage and high-speed mobile legibility.

- **Headlines (Vollkorn):** Sturdy, organic serif with rounded bracket serifs and warm, literary proportions. Headlines celebrate key artisan statements, section titles, and signature dessert callouts. Use italic styling sparingly on display titles to emphasize morning bake cycles and handmade heritage.
- **Body & Functional UI (Karla):** A grotesque sans-serif with subtle warm idiosyncrasies. It delivers exceptional readability for dish descriptions, ingredient provenance, price tags, and ordering controls on dense mobile screens.
- **Micro-labels & Tags:** Uppercase `label-tag` tokens are used strictly for status callouts (e.g., *HORNEADO HOY*, *VEGANO*, *MASA MADRE*).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around mobile-first QR consumption and desktop digital showcase experiences.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column fluid stream with horizontal scrolling category chips. Edge padding is `margin` (`1rem`) to maximize card real estate.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid, ideal for side-by-side product cards and split cart views.
  - **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column max-width container (`1200px`) centered with generous `margin-desktop` (`3.5rem`).
- **Vertical Rhythm:**
  - Product item listings maintain a strict `space-md` gap.
  - Section blocks and culinary categories transition with `space-xl` on mobile and `space-2xl` on desktop to allow photographic bakery imagery to breathe.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tactile surface warmth and soft, tinted ambient illumination rather than harsh tech dropshadows.

- **Ground Level (Canvas):** The base background `#FDF6ED` serves as the canvas.
- **Level 1 (Menu Cards & Interactive Containers):** Raised cards use `#FFFFFF` with a subtle warm border (`1px solid #E7DACB`) and an ambient shadow tinted with warm espresso: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(55, 42, 32, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Cart Bar & Active Sheets):** Sticky floating bottom order bars and active QR menus use `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -4px rgba(55, 42, 32, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Bottom Sheets & Overlays):** Modal sheets for item selection and ingredient customizations blur backdrops with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` and cast a deep ground shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(55, 42, 32, 0.25)`.
- **Inverted Dark Surfaces:** Deep roast blocks (`#372A20` and `#2A1F17`) provide high-contrast grounding for featured bakery specials and footers without elevation shadows.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary mimics the organic, soft contours of hand-shaped bread, brioche, and rolled pastries.

- **Default Radius (`0.5rem` / `rounded-md`):** Input fields, nutritional notes, and small product thumbnail frames.
- **Card & Hero Radius (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`):** Product dish cards, split showcase blocks, and category cards.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px` / Full Pill):** Floating action buttons, category filter tabs, cart counter indicators, and status badges.
- **Bottom Sheet Top Curvature (`1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`):** Mobile customization drawers and order summaries feature curved upper corners for a warm, sheet-like feel.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Order Button:** Amber background (`#D97706`), bold white text, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Hover state transitions to `#B45309`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Espresso outline (`1.5px solid #372A20`), espresso text, transparent or linen background.
- **Floating Cart Button:** Deep espresso (`#2A1F17`) pill badge with white text, live item counter badge in `#D97706`, and subtle price divider.
- **WhatsApp Action Button:** Bright WhatsApp green (`#22C55E`), white icon and text, tactile tap bounce on mobile.

### Menu Dish Cards
- Crisp white surface (`#FFFFFF`) framed by `1px solid #E7DACB` and `1rem` corner radius.
- Imagery spans the top or left with a `4:3` aspect ratio, treated with natural warm lighting.
- Card title rendered in `headline-sm` (`Vollkorn`) with price in bold amber (`#D97706`).
- Quick-add button positioned at the bottom right corner as a circular or pill plus-control.

### Category Chips & Tabs
- Horizontal scrollable filter bar with hidden scrollbars.
- **Inactive:** Linen surface (`#F5ECE1`), dark brown text, soft warm border.
- **Active:** Deep espresso fill (`#372A20`), white text, no border.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF4EC`, bordered in `#E7DACB`.
- Focused state highlights border in `#D97706` with `0 0 0 3px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.15)`.
- Typography is clean `Karla` regular with deep espresso value text.

### Badges & Nutrition Chips
- Micro tags for *Masa Madre*, *Recién Horneado*, and *Sin Gluten*.
- Pill-shaped with `0.25rem 0.625rem` padding, subtle warm beige fill (`#F5ECE1`), and dark espresso uppercase lettering (`label-tag`).
